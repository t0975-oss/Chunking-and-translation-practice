#1

有些人反對選美比賽，因為這些比賽提倡人們需要苗條才能美麗的思想。

Some people oppose beauty contests because these contests promote the idea that people need to be slim to be beautiful.

時態：現在簡單式（陳述普遍現象）。句型：S + oppose + O + because + S + promote the idea that + 同位語子句（說明 idea 的具體內容）。

語塊步驟： 1. 選美比賽 beauty contests 2. 反對選美比賽 oppose beauty contests 3. 需要苗條才能美麗 need to be slim to be beautiful 4. 提倡人們需要苗條才能美麗的思想 promote the idea that people need to be slim to be beautiful
#2

觀看這些比賽的人可能會過度在乎自己的身材，並嘗試以極端的方式減重。

Those who watch these contests may care too much about their figure and try to lose weight in extreme ways.

時態：現在簡單式 + 助動詞 may（表推測可能）。句型：Those who + V（凡是…的人）+ may + V1 and V2（對等動詞連接兩個動作）。

語塊步驟： 1. 觀看這些比賽的人 Those who watch these contests 2. 過度在乎自己的身材 care too much about their figure 3. 以極端的方式減重 lose weight in extreme ways 4. 嘗試以極端的方式減重 try to lose weight in extreme ways
#3

任何曾照顧過小孩的人就會知道養育小孩不簡單。

Anyone who has cared for a child knows that parenting is not easy.

時態：關係子句用現在完成式（has cared for，強調「曾經」的經驗），主要子句用現在簡單式（knows，單數主詞 Anyone 動詞加 s）。

語塊步驟： 1. 照顧過小孩 has cared for a child 2. 任何曾照顧過小孩的人 Anyone who has cared for a child 3. 養育小孩不簡單 parenting is not easy
#4

許多專家提倡家長應該更注意孩子的心理發展，而不只是滿足他們的生理需求。

Many experts promote that parents should pay more attention to their children's psychological development rather than just meeting their physical needs.

時態：現在簡單式 + should。句型：promote that + S + (should) + V ... rather than + V-ing（而不是…；rather than 後接動名詞與前面動作對比）。

語塊步驟： 1. 心理發展 psychological development 2. 更注意孩子的心理發展 pay more attention to their children's psychological development 3. 滿足他們的生理需求 meeting their physical needs 4. 而不只是滿足他們的生理需求 rather than just meeting their physical needs
#5

格雷琴(Gretchen)不認同她爸對她男友身材所做的無禮評論。

Gretchen opposed the rude comments that her father made about her boyfriend's figure.

時態：過去簡單式（opposed / made，敘述過去發生的事件）。句型：S + opposed + N + that + S + made about...（關係子句修飾 comments）。

語塊步驟： 1. 無禮的評論 the rude comments 2. 對她男友的身材 about her boyfriend's figure 3. 她爸對她男友身材所做的無禮評論 the rude comments that her father made about her boyfriend's figure
#6

他說他太胖，配不上他苗條美麗的女兒。

He said he was too fat for his slim, beautiful daughter.

時態：過去簡單式（主句 said 為過去式，名詞子句時態一致用 was）。句型：be + too + adj. + for + sb.（對某人來說太…而配不上）。

語塊步驟： 1. 太胖 too fat 2. 他苗條美麗的女兒 his slim, beautiful daughter 3. 太胖而配不上他苗條美麗的女兒 too fat for his slim, beautiful daughter
#7

因為日益增長的新住民家庭數量，臺灣政府已將七種東南亞語言加入學校課綱。

Because of the growing number of new immigrant families, the Taiwanese government has added seven Southeast Asian languages to the school curriculum.

時態：現在完成式（has added，表示政府「已經」完成且影響至今的政策）。句型：Because of + 名詞片語, S + has added + A + to + B。

語塊步驟： 1. 新住民家庭 new immigrant families 2. 日益增長的新住民家庭數量 the growing number of new immigrant families 3. 七種東南亞語言 seven Southeast Asian languages 4. 將七種東南亞語言加入學校課綱 added seven Southeast Asian languages to the school curriculum
#8

這項決定不僅會讓學生具備更好的語言技能，還會讓他們熟悉該區域的不同文化。

This decision will not only equip students with better language skills, but also acquaint them with the region's different cultures.

時態：未來簡單式（will + 原形動詞）。句型：not only + V1 ..., but also + V2 ...（不僅…而且…，連接兩個對等的原形動詞片語）。

語塊步驟： 1. 更好的語言技能 better language skills 2. 讓學生具備更好的語言技能 equip students with better language skills 3. 該區域的不同文化 the region's different cultures 4. 讓他們熟悉該區域的不同文化 acquaint them with the region's different cultures
#9

哈利(Harry)開始新工作的頭幾天都花時間在熟悉公司結構和政策。

Harry spent the first few days at his new job acquainting himself with the company's structure and policies.

時態：過去簡單式（spent）。句型：S + spend + 時間 + V-ing（花時間做某事）；acquaint oneself with + N（使自己熟悉…）。

語塊步驟： 1. 開始新工作的頭幾天 the first few days at his new job 2. 公司結構和政策 the company's structure and policies 3. 熟悉公司結構和政策 acquainting himself with the company's structure and policies
#10

身為部門經理，他不僅要監督下屬目前的工作進度，還要制定明年的工作計畫。

As a department manager, he not only supervises his subordinates' current work progress, but also makes plans for the next year.

時態：現在簡單式（描述職務常態，第三人稱單數動詞加 s）。句型：As + 身分, S + not only + V1s ..., but also + V2s ...。

語塊步驟： 1. 身為部門經理 As a department manager 2. 下屬目前的工作進度 his subordinates' current work progress 3. 監督下屬目前的工作進度 supervises his subordinates' current work progress 4. 制定明年的工作計畫 makes plans for the next year
#11

舊課綱注重學生對知識的理解，而新課綱旨在讓學生具備將其應用在真實生活中的能力。

While the old curriculum focused on students' understanding of the knowledge, the new one aims to equip students with the ability to apply it in real life.

時態：前半句用過去簡單式（focused on 舊課綱），後半句用現在簡單式（aims to 新課綱）。句型：While + 子句1, 子句2（對比「然而」）；aim to + V。

語塊步驟： 1. 學生對知識的理解 students' understanding of the knowledge 2. 注重學生對知識的理解 focused on students' understanding of the knowledge 3. 將其應用在真實生活中的能力 the ability to apply it in real life 4. 讓學生具備將其應用在真實生活中的能力 equip students with the ability to apply it in real life
#12

一些教育學者對新課綱有疑慮，他們擔心學生的表現將無法公平地評量。

Some educators have doubts about the new curriculum and are worried that students' performance will not be fairly assessed.

時態：現在簡單式（have doubts / are worried）+ 未來被動式（will not be fairly assessed，表現「被」評量）。

語塊步驟： 1. 對新課綱有疑慮 have doubts about the new curriculum 2. 學生的表現 students' performance 3. 將無法公平地評量 will not be fairly assessed
#13

受到低生育率的影響，許多學校正被迫尋求創新的方法以吸引學生。

Affected by the low birth rates, many schools are being forced to seek out innovative ways to attract students.

時態：現在進行被動式（are being forced to + V，強調「正被迫」）。句型：分詞構句 Affected by + N（受到…影響）, S + V...。

語塊步驟： 1. 受到低生育率的影響 Affected by the low birth rates 2. 創新的方法 innovative ways 3. 尋求創新的方法以吸引學生 seek out innovative ways to attract students 4. 正被迫尋求創新的方法 are being forced to seek out innovative ways
#14

有些學校與公司合作，提供學生機會以習得專業技能。

Some schools and companies work together to provide students with opportunities to acquire professional skills.

時態：現在簡單式。句型：work together to + V（合作以…）；provide + 人 + with + 物（提供某人某物）+ to V（不定詞修飾 opportunities）。

語塊步驟： 1. 與公司合作 work together 2. 習得專業技能 acquire professional skills 3. 提供學生機會以習得專業技能 provide students with opportunities to acquire professional skills
#15

知道我對於被要求做的工作毫無經驗，我尋求一位同事的幫忙。

Knowing I had no experience with the work I was asked to do, I sought out the help of a colleague.

時態：過去簡單式（had / was asked / sought，注意 seek 的過去式為 sought）。句型：分詞構句 Knowing (that) + 子句, S + sought out + O。

語塊步驟： 1. 被要求做的工作 the work I was asked to do 2. 對於被要求做的工作毫無經驗 had no experience with the work I was asked to do 3. 尋求一位同事的幫忙 sought out the help of a colleague
#16

詹姆士(James)提供了我一些上一個專案裡使用的文件。

James provided me with some documents used in the last project.

時態：過去簡單式（provided）。句型：provide + 人 + with + 物；過去分詞片語 used in the last project（被使用的文件）後位修飾 documents。

語塊步驟： 1. 上一個專案裡使用的 used in the last project 2. 一些上一個專案裡使用的文件 some documents used in the last project 3. 提供了我一些文件 provided me with some documents
#17

我們公司正與一個時尚品牌合作，以行銷我們的最新商品。

Our company is collaborating with a fashion brand to market our latest products.

時態：現在進行式（is collaborating，表示「正」在進行的合作）。句型：collaborate with + 對象 + to V（與…合作以達成某目的）。

語塊步驟： 1. 一個時尚品牌 a fashion brand 2. 正與一個時尚品牌合作 is collaborating with a fashion brand 3. 行銷我們的最新商品 market our latest products
#18

我們將提供首次購買的特別折扣來吸引新顧客。

We will offer special discounts on first purchases to attract new customers.

時態：未來簡單式（will offer）。句型：offer + 折扣 + on + 消費項目 + to V（不定詞表目的「來吸引…」）。

語塊步驟： 1. 特別折扣 special discounts 2. 首次購買的特別折扣 special discounts on first purchases 3. 吸引新顧客 attract new customers
